# Hossein Teimouri

Integrated PhD student in Biotechnology at the University of Tehran,
working on machine learning for therapeutic peptide and protein design.

Interests: sequence-based prediction of protein biophysical behaviour,
generative models for peptide design, and computational approaches to
drug discovery.

---


### Research

**Phaseek** — generalizable prediction of liquid–liquid phase separation
from protein sequence, with gradient-based generation of de novo
phase-separating peptides, experimentally validated in *E. coli*.
A Transformer sequence encoder combined with FEGS graph matrices.
Co-first author, with the Lindner and Pandi groups at INSERM /
Université Paris Cité.

Preprint: [bioRxiv 10.1101/2025.01.27.635039](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.01.27.635039v2) ·
Code: [AMIRMOHAMMAD-OSS/Phaseek](https://github.com/AMIRMOHAMMAD-OSS/Phaseek) ·
Under review.

### Projects

**[sgo-heart-disease-reproduction](https://github.com/t-hossein/sgo-heart-disease-reproduction)**
Reproduction of an SGO-tuned RF/XGBoost framework (Naik et al., *Sci Rep*
2025) from the paper's text, no public implementation existing. The
search's fitness function scores candidates on the test set; measuring
that directly gives about 10 accuracy points of optimism, while held-out
accuracy is unchanged once the objective is fixed. Adds a multi-fidelity
gate that cuts model fits per search by roughly a third.

**[ypfh-esterase-solubility-design](https://github.com/t-hossein/ypfh-esterase-solubility-design)**
Computational redesign of the *E. coli* YpfH esterase to reduce
aggregation propensity without disturbing the Ser–His–Asp catalytic
relay. 37 candidate substitutions scored on Aggrescan3D, FoldX, CamSol,
DynaMut and SASA exposure, filtered to three designs in the disordered
C-terminal tail. AlphaFold3, MolProbity, PyMOL/APBS, Clustal Omega.

**[parp1-myelosuppression](https://github.com/t-hossein/parp1-myelosuppression)**
Why PARP inhibitors cause bone marrow suppression: high PARP1 dependence
in haematopoietic stem cells set against comparatively low expression of
the homologous-recombination machinery they would need to fall back on.
Built from CellxGene, Expression Atlas, Reactome, DepMap/CCLE, DisGeNET
and GEO.
